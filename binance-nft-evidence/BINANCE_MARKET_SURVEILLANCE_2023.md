## Why this matters to the August 2026 evidence

These emails do **not** prove that the August 2026 account labels are linked, automated, or Binance-operated.

They establish a narrower but important point: Binance itself had an articulated NFT market-surveillance standard in 2023 that treated coordinated/pre-arranged trading intended to influence **price or volume** as prohibited wash trading / market manipulation.

The August 2026 public dataset should therefore be evaluated against that stated standard using Binance-controlled evidence such as KYC linkage, device/IP linkage, funding sources, matching-engine records and internal surveillance alerts.
